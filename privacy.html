Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Who I am

This website is the personal portfolio of Ahmed Berrechach, a freelance web developer based in Beni Mellal, Morocco. I am the person responsible for the personal data described on this page.

Contact: ahmed.berrechach1@gmail.com

2. What data I collect

I only collect the information you choose to send me when you contact me by email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the content of your message

If you contact me on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or by email, I receive the information those services share with me, such as your name, phone number or profile.

3. Why I use it

I use your data only to read your message, reply to you, and prepare a quote or carry out a project you ask for. I do not use it for advertising, I do not sell it and I do not share it with anyone for marketing.

The legal basis is your consent (you send the message yourself) and, if we start working together, the steps needed before and during a contract.

4. How you can contact me

The website has no database and no active contact form. If you write to me by email, your message is received in my inbox (Gmail). If you use WhatsApp or LinkedIn, your message goes through those services, under their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics tools. Fonts are hosted on the site itself, so no request is sent to font providers when you visit.

Your language and theme choices are saved in your browser (local storage) only, so the site remembers them on your next visit.

6. External links

The site links to LinkedIn and WhatsApp. When you click those links you leave this site and their own privacy policies apply.

7. How long I keep your data

Messages are kept for as long as needed to answer you and, if we work together, for the length of the project and the period required to keep business records. You can ask me to delete your messages at any time.

8. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to access, correct or delete the data I hold about you, to restrict its use, or to withdraw your consent. Just write to ahmed.berrechach1@gmail.com and I will answer as soon as possible.

If you live in Morocco, you also have the rights provided by Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, and you may contact the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel). If you live in the European Union, you may contact your local data protection authority.

9. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so please do not send passwords or sensitive personal data in your messages.

10. Children

This website is not intended for children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
